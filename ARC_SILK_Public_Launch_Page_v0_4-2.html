Frequently asked

Clear by design.

Is ARC_SILK free to join?

Basic access and discovery are intended to be free. Eligible purchases carry a disclosed 5% platform commission.

Does buying a badge guarantee verification?

No. Payment is reviewed separately from identity and professional eligibility. Only approved professionals receive an active badge.

Will my exact address be public?

No exact home address is required by this design. Seller profiles use optional coarse location and remote availability.

Can customers who only buy services purchase a professional badge?

No. Paid professional badge applications are limited to eligible music-industry roles.
