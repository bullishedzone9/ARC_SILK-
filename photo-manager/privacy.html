Privacy Policy

This page explains how Pluginflex Photo Manager handles information when you use the photo-library website.

In brief: the library is designed so each signed-in user sees their own photos. Do not upload anything you would not want stored by an online service.

Information processed

  • Account information used to sign in, such as your email address.
  • Photos you choose to upload.
  • Optional captions and category labels you save.
  • Technical information processed by the website and its hosting/authentication providers to operate and secure the service.

Where information is stored

The website uses Supabase for authentication, photo storage, and photo metadata, and Vercel for website hosting. Uploaded photos are stored in a private storage bucket; the app is designed to request access only for the signed-in account. The service may use temporary signed links for certain photo operations, which expire after a limited period.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, store and display your photos, remember labels and captions, and maintain service security. This draft does not promise that the service has completed an independent security audit.

Sharing

Pluginflex Photo Manager is not designed to publish your library publicly. Infrastructure providers process data to operate the service. Do not assume that a photo is saved, shared, or deleted unless the website confirms the action.

Retention and deletion

Photos and metadata may remain stored until you delete them using available controls or the service operator performs maintenance. The current website provides photo deletion, but this page does not promise a self-service account-deletion feature. If you need help with account or data deletion, use the support page.

Children

The service is not specifically designed for children. A parent or guardian should supervise any child's use and uploads.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the service develops. For questions or data requests, follow the support instructions. A request is not complete until you receive confirmation.
