Terms of Use

These terms describe the current early-stage ARC_SILK Photos website.

Using the service

You may use the website to store and organise photos in one collection using labels and captions. The service is planned to be free at launch; features, limits, and availability may change as it develops.

Your account and uploads

  • Keep your sign-in details private and use an email address you control.
  • Upload only content you own or have permission to store.
  • Do not upload unlawful content, violate another person's privacy, or attempt to access another user's library.
  • Check that a photo has uploaded successfully before relying on it as your only copy.

Privacy and availability

Read the Privacy Policy to understand the service's current data handling. The service is provided on an evolving, best-effort basis; uninterrupted availability and permanent preservation of files are not guaranteed. Keep a separate backup of important photos.

Features and pricing

The current plan is to offer the service free at launch. Any future paid features or pricing changes should be communicated before they apply to you.

Suspension and changes

Access may be limited to protect the service, investigate misuse, or perform maintenance. Features and these terms may change as the product develops.

Questions

For help or requests, follow the instructions on the Support page.
